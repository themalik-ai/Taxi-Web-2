Good to Know

Your Questions, Answered

How do I book a chauffeur in the UK or Dubai?

You can book directly through this website using our reservation form, via WhatsApp, or by calling our 24/7 concierge team. Every booking is confirmed personally by a dedicated account manager.

What types of vehicles are in your fleet?

Our fleet includes the Mercedes S-Class, E-Class, V-Class & EQV, BMW 7 Series, Range Rover, and on request, Bentleys and Rolls-Royces. Every vehicle is under three years old and meticulously maintained.

Are your chauffeurs professionally trained?

Yes. All of our chauffeurs are strictly vetted, professionally trained in defensive and courteous driving, and sign strict non-disclosure agreements to protect your privacy.

Do you offer airport meet & greet services?

Absolutely. We track your flight in real time, provide a complimentary meet & greet inside the terminal, and include up to 60 minutes of free waiting time for airport collections.

Is there a minimum booking duration?

By-the-hour bookings have a minimum of 3 hours. Airport transfers and point-to-point journeys are priced per route. Full-day hire (8 hours) is available at a preferential day rate.

Are there any hidden fees?

Never. Our pricing is fully transparent — no surge charges, no booking fees, no surprises. What we quote is what you pay.

Can I open a corporate account?

Yes. We offer corporate accounts with a dedicated account manager, priority booking, monthly consolidated invoicing and tailored travel programmes for your executives and guests.

What is your cancellation policy?

Complimentary cancellation is available up to 24 hours before your scheduled pick-up. For events and wedding bookings, our concierge will outline flexible terms at the time of reservation.
